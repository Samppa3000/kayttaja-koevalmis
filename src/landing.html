Questions

Is Koevalmis the official test or from Migri?

No. Koevalmis is an independent practice service. The official test and its study material come from the Finnish Immigration Service (Migri). Always check the official information on migri.fi.

Are these the real test questions?

No. We write our own questions from public information on the topics the law lists. When Migri publishes the official study material around the turn of the year, we update the questions to match it.

Which languages are available?

Questions are in Finnish with English translations. More languages are being added before the course opens.

Do I have to take the test?

It depends. The requirement applies to applications from 1 March 2027 for people aged 18–64. A Finnish or Swedish matriculation exam, or a Finnish- or Swedish-language degree completed in Finland, can replace it. Migri decides on exemptions.
